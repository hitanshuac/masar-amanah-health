# SOP-05: Iatrogenic 74-Day TRT/Finasteride Audit vs. Occult Pituitary Adenoma Escalation

**Protocol ID:** `SOP-05`  
**Target Archetype:** `MALAFFI-PT-002` (TRT Iatrogenic Shutdown)  
**Clinical Category:** Endocrine Discrimination & Iatrogenic Suppression  
**Encounter Phase:** Day 0 Intake / Audit  
**Framework:** Universal 4-Step S-O-A-P Clinical Encounter  

---

## 1. Clinical Purpose & Rationale
Exogenous testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS), and selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs) exert profound negative feedback on the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis. By suppressing hypothalamic GnRH pulsatility and pituitary LH/FSH secretion to undetectable levels ($<0.2\text{ IU/L}$), intratesticular testosterone levels plummet below physiological requirements ($>1000\text{ ng/dL}$ intratesticularly required for spermatogenesis), resulting in profound azoospermia.

However, an identical laboratory pattern of low LH and FSH can be produced by central sellar compressive lesions, such as **prolactin-secreting pituitary macroadenomas**. This SOP delineates the critical clinical audit steps to confirm iatrogenic suppression, rule out pituitary lesions, and establish the 74-day physiological recovery protocol.

---

## 2. 4-Step S-O-A-P Workflow

### S: Subjective Intake & Androgen Audit
- **Anamnesis:** Detailed audit of over-the-counter gym bulking powders, peptide injections, testosterone cypionate/enanthate, and SARMs.
- **Neurological & Sellar Screen:** Specific interrogation for persistent retro-orbital headaches, loss of peripheral vision (bitemporal hemianopsia from optic chiasm compression), and galactorrhea.
- **Symptoms:** Bilateral testicular shrinkage (hypotrophy) and erectile dysfunction following abrupt supplement cessation.
- **Gate Check:** `[✓ Verify TRT Exposure & Pituitary Red-Flag Screen]`.

### O: Objective QC & Gonadotropin Arrest
- **Centrifuged Semen Analysis:** Complete Azoospermia ($0.0\times 10^6\text{ /mL}$) confirmed after high-speed centrifugation (3000g for 15 min per WHO 6th Ed).
- **Hormonal Profile (Central Suppression):**
  - Serum LH: $<0.2\text{ IU/L}$ (Profoundly suppressed)
  - Serum FSH: $<0.2\text{ IU/L}$ (Profoundly suppressed)
  - Total Testosterone: $140\text{ ng/dL}$ (Post-cessation nadir)
  - **Serum Prolactin:** $8.2\text{ ng/mL}$ (Strictly Normal, $<20\text{ ng/mL}$) $\rightarrow$ Crucial negative discriminator for prolactinoma.

### A: Assessment & Etiological Distinction
- **Clinical Rule Triggered:** Rule `RES-7` (Anabolic Steroids / TRT HPG Axis Suppression).
- **Primary Diagnosis:** Iatrogenic Hypogonadotropic Hypogonadism (`ICD-10 E23.0`).
- **Certainty Level:** $96.2\%$ diagnostic confidence.
- **Pituitary Escalation Check:** Ruled out `ESC_PITUITARY_MASS` due to strictly normal Prolactin and absence of visual field constriction.

### P: Plan & Recovery Regimen
- **Triage Level:** **TIER_2_ANDROLOGY_SPECIALIST**.
- **Cessation Protocol:** Immediate and complete discontinuation of all exogenous androgens, SARMs, and unverified gym supplements.
- **Pharmacotherapy Reboot Pathway:** Specialist-guided medical induction of spermatogenesis using human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG 1500–2000 IU subcutaneous 2–3 times weekly) $\pm$ selective estrogen receptor modulators (Clomiphene citrate 25–50mg daily).
- **Longitudinal Horizon:** Establish strict 74-day baseline (duration of one spermatogenic maturation cycle). Repeat semen analysis scheduled at $+90\text{ days}$.

---

## 3. Exception & Deviation Handling
- **Deviation (Elevated Prolactin >50 ng/mL or Visual Loss):** If Prolactin is elevated and/or patient reports peripheral visual deficit, cancel outpatient reboot and activate immediate **STAT Pituitary Sellar MRI (`ESC_PITUITARY_MASS`)** and neuro-endocrinology referral at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi.
- **Deviation (Patient Black-Market PCT Self-Medication):** Warn against illicit online "post-cycle therapy" products, which frequently contain hepatotoxic oral steroids or adulterants.
